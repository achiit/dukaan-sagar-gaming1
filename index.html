About

A gaming zone made for local kids

I started Sagar Gaming because my cousin and I had nowhere decent to play in Charkhi Dadri. We used to travel to another town just to try a PS5. I wanted a place here where every player feels at home.

I am at the counter myself most days. If a controller is not working or the match lags, tell me and I fix it. Parents are welcome to come and see the place first.

  • Owner always present, no hidden charges
  • Clean seating, fans and cooling
  • Regular Free Fire tournaments
  • Safe environment for students
  • Book by phone or WhatsApp
